# Riftbound — first-spell visual concepts

Reference concept art generated with the built-in image generation tool. This sheet establishes art direction; it is not an animation atlas. First-slot effects are implemented procedurally in src/first-spell-art.js. The original sheet predates the revisions below: the implementation uses one Wind Blade and one primary Stone Spike.

Image: [first-spell-designs.png](first-spell-designs.png)
Style reference: the current Riftbound game screenshot, with its compact wizard, wooden staff, charcoal-purple arena and elemental clothing colors.

## Generation brief

Create a detailed dark-fantasy pixel-art concept sheet for exactly five first-slot abilities. Keep the current compact wizard design, use distinct elemental materials and restrained directional particles, and show every effect at peak action. Use crisp stepped silhouettes, layered shading, controlled pixel light spill and a small palette per effect. Include small spell titles, but no combat HUD, statistics, animation frames or extra attacks.

- Fire / Flame Pillar: vertical orange-red eruption beneath a target, white-yellow hot core, curling flame tongues, scorched ground and ascending embers.
- Earth / Stone Spikes: one large jagged faceted rock spike with four smaller spikes around its base, connected cracks, stone chips and low dust puffs. Stone rather than magical crystal.
- Water / Ice Lance: one piercing crystalline ice spear, pale cyan facets, deep-blue edges, white glints, cold vapor wake and frost shards. Ice rather than a water stream.
- Lightning / Thunder Strike: a descending blue-white bolt, jagged violet secondary branches, contact burst, electrical fragments and scorched ground.
- Wind / Wind Blade: exactly one compressed-air crescent, mint-white razor edges, translucent teal ribbons, curving wakes and sparse drifting streaks.

Keep the main effects readable. Particles should support material motion rather than obscure the wizard or attack silhouette. Use the same base wizard in all five studies, with only elemental clothing/orb colors changing. The staff remains wooden and in the anatomical right hand. Avoid realistic painting, 3D rendering, photographic bloom, excessive glitter, blood/gore, watermarks and modern UI.
