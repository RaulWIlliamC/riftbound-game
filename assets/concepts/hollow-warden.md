# The Hollow Warden

Boss concept for Whispering Ruins. Concept only; not implemented in gameplay.

An ancient shrine guardian has mistaken every living visitor for an intruder. Roots bind broken courtyard pillars into its body, while a trapped rune spirit powers its hollow chest. Its stone bell arm once called worshippers to the shrine; now it tolls before every heavy slam.

## Appearance

Broad, hunched shoulders made from broken fluted pillars; a hollow stone mask with amber eyes; an asymmetrical bell-shaped hammer arm; thick root bindings and olive moss. A recessed cyan rune core distinguishes it from the ordinary Moss Guardian. Target scale: roughly three wizard heights, with a compact readable silhouette.

## Encounter proposal

- **Root Surge:** roots advance along three visibly marked ground lanes. Dash through a gap or move between the lanes. Roots follow the terrain perspective and pass behind scenery.
- **Shrine Slam:** the bell arm rises, tolls once and slams a locked elliptical footprint. Stone fractures and dust spread along the ground. A long recovery offers a damage window.
- **Wisp Procession:** three rune wisps orbit the guardian and release staggered, dodgeable bolts. Defeating them removes its temporary protection.
- **Core Exposed:** after two heavy slams, chest plates separate for three seconds, exposing the cyan spirit and increasing damage taken.

At half health, moss sheds and root bindings glow. Root Surge gains a delayed second wave, but retains the same advance warning. Shrine Slam still exposes the core; difficulty comes from overlapping attacks, not shorter unreadable wind-ups.

Arena: the ruined circular shrine courtyard, with enough open space to read lanes and avoid attacks. Preserve the map's quiet forest palette; use amber for warnings and cyan for the vulnerable core. Boss resists capture and displacement.

Possible reward: Warden's Rune, an area-clear progression reward, and a restored shrine visual. Values and rewards require later gameplay tuning.
